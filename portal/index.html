The stick is sliding on its own. The MPU-6050 was not found. Check 3.3V, GND, SDA on GPIO 8, and SCL on GPIO 9.

Plug the ESP32 in with USB-C, press RESET, hold the wheel still for a few seconds, then turn it. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are not used.

If the wheel stays at 0, click Connect USB and choose ESP32S3_DEV. Open this page in Chrome at localhost. The same axis is drawn as an attitude gyro.
